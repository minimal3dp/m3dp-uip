1. Introduction
3-D printing is one among the advanced method of additive
manufacturing (AM) for making various range of products. The
method comprises of printing continuous layers one among
another. AM is most commonly adopted method in various engi-
neering applications [1]. Techniques of AM have been developed
comprehensively with various types of materials and it is a beneﬁ-
cial approach for making prototypes, tools and other components
[2]. Fusion Deposition Modelling [FDM] is a methodology which
using similar to traditional method such as injection moulding.
This method of making components involves molten plastic which
is extruded from a hot nozzle for making the part layer one above
another [3]. FDM is the most commonly engaged method due to its
ease of use and economic beneﬁts and also it is the method to fab-
ricate functional parts without any difﬁculty in achieving geomet-
rical requirements [4–6]. Polyactic Acid (PLA) is one of the most
auspicious material that is biodegradable and it can be the suitable
alternate for traditionally available plastics. Also the exceptional
tensile characteristics of these materials laid a pathway as best
opted for medical and other mild structural applications. Packaging
industries have extensive usage of PLA material due to its
biodegradable characteristics [7–11]. Response Surface Methodol-
ogy has been engaged for analyzing the process parameters and
also for deriving the relationship amidst the considered input
and output variables with the help of empirical equations. Also
the effect of process parameters on desired output variables are
also assessed [12–14].An investigation has been performed for
optimizing the mechanical characteristics using Taguchi based
grey approach. The interaction effect among the process parame-
ters have been derived and it is signiﬁcant process parameters
were identiﬁed [15].
AM is one of the most favorable method for making PLA with
needed structure. Since the traditional approaches for not offering
required performance expected by various manufacturers. Carbon
nanoparticles are most considered as reinforcement due to its bio-
compatibility for various industrial applications [16–19]. Taguchi’s
approach is one of the important and proﬁcient method for exper-
iment design and it is most suitable for optimizing the parameters
with single objectives. Multiple process parameters have been
optimized with the help of Multi Criteria Decision Making (MCDM)

2. Materials and methods
PLA is one of the environmental friendly material and also hav-
ing better biodegradability. In this present investigation, PLA is
considered as work material and fed through the FDM setup which
is illustrated in Fig. 1. PLA which is taken as wire are fused and
made to deposit as layer one among another. The thickness and
size of the layer have been predetermined and fed into the FDM
setup. The submicron sized carbon particles have been selected
for achieving the desired mechanical characteristics. During the
time of fabricating the layers, an attachment also provided for
depositing the carbon particles on the surface. The desired work
samples are made in accordance with ‘‘ASTM D638 TYPE V”
standard.
Taguchi’s experimental design is adopted for reducing the com-
plexity while designing the experimental trials. It is a proven
approach for making effective experimental design and also for
analyzing the process parameters. Based on the literature three
factors with three levels are selected as shown in Table 1 and as
per Taguchi’s approach an L27 Orthogonal Array has been selected
for conducting experiments. By varying the process parameters as
per selected OA, 27 parts are printed. The temperature of extruder
and bet are maintained as 240 C and 100 C respectively, through-
out the experimentation. Tensile strength, toughness and surface
roughness of printed samples are considered as performance mea-
sures in this investigation.

9. Conclusions
 FDM process has been adopted for fabricating the in-situ PLA
polymer with carbon particles. Single aspects optimization by
Taguchi’s analysis has been used for determining the best pro-
cess parameters to attain optimal single performance.
 Tensile strength of the fabricated component is inﬂuence by
printing speed and then it is followed by carbon deposition
and number of layers.
 The toughness of fabricated samples is predominantly inﬂu-
enced by printing speed and then it is trailed by carbon deposi-
tion and number of layers.
 Carbon deposition is the major parameter that inﬂuences the
surface roughness of the fabricated component and then it is
trailed by number of layers and printing speed.
 GRA method has been engaged for ascertaining the multi per-
formance index and the optimum process variables for attaining
multiple performance are derived. According the GRA approach,
printing speed is the signiﬁcant process variable that inﬂuences
the overall performance of the selected FDM process.
 The interaction effect of process variables are revealed by inter-
action analysis. Most the considered performance measures are
having interaction effect with all parameters at different levels.
