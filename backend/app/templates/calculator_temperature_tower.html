📖 How to Use Temperature Tower

Temperature towers test multiple temperatures in a single print to find the optimal temperature for your specific filament brand/color.

What to look for:

  • Surface finish: Smooth, glossy finish without roughness
  • Stringing: Minimal or no strings between features
  • Bridging: Clean bridges without sagging
  • Overhangs: Sharp overhangs without drooping
  • Layer adhesion: Layers bond well, no delamination

Tips:

  • Print towers in 5°C increments for good resolution
  • Use calipers to measure height accurately
  • Test both functional and aesthetic aspects
  • Results vary by filament brand and even color!
